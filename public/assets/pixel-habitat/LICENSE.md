## Seaside house scene

`seaside-house.png` is the static fallback rendered from
`src/components/home/seasideTreehouse.ts`. The architecture, landscape, furniture,
and ambient animation are drawn locally in Canvas; small decorative sprites use
wareya's CC0 tile pack above. The supplied reference screenshot is not embedded.

## 3D coastal habitat

`coastal-3d.png` is a render of the original procedural Three.js scene in
`src/components/home/voxelHabitat.ts`. Its geometry and colors are generated
locally; this scene uses no Minecraft, Stonehearth, or third-party art assets.
